Solutions by industry

The same robots, edge AI and sensors, put to work on the problems of seven industries. Pick yours.

  • Smart ports

    Track assets across the terminal, keep cranes working and cut fuel use, with sensors and edge AI that run on site.

  • Public cleaning and maintenance

    Clean streets and public spaces when they need it, not on a fixed round, using cameras on trucks and a robotic arm that does the work.

  • Facility management

    Keep restrooms clean through the whole cycle with a cleaning robot your team can watch and control remotely in a 3D view.

  • Waste management

    Know which bins need emptying, spot illegal dumping and hazardous waste, and plan collection routes from live data.

  • High-precision manufacturing

    Bring Industry 4.0 to the factory floor with connected machines, digital twins and mobile robots you can run remotely.

  • Public infrastructure monitoring

    Find corrosion, cracks and leaks on vehicles and mobile structures early, before they turn into failures.

  • Smart occupancy monitoring

    See how spaces are used, how people move through them and where assets are, from tags and sensors with machine learning.
