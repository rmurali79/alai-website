What we build

Robotics

Autonomous mobile robots and robotic arms that map their surroundings with LIDAR and work beside people.

  • Autonomous mobile robots
  • Machine vision for robotic arms
  • Arms retrofitted onto vehicles

Edge AI

Models built for one job and run on low-power hardware on site, sized for the compute, network, power and heat they will meet.

  • Custom models, from problem to deployment
  • Industrial edge server sizing
  • Object detection and volume measurement

Industrial IoT

Outdoor-rated sensors and secure networks that show where assets are and when they need attention.

  • IP67 sensors, UWB, BLE and active tags
  • Predictive maintenance and live monitoring
  • Remote operation through 3D digital twins
